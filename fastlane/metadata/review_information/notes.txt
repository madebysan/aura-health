Aura is an iPhone-only, local-first personal health organizer. No login or subscription is required.

Apple Health access is optional and read-only. Reviewers may skip Apple Health and use the built-in sample-data path during onboarding.

AI is optional. The app remains useful without an API key. If a reviewer wants to inspect AI setup, Settings shows provider selection, user-supplied API key storage, model selection, and explicit provider-specific consent. No health information or attachment is sent automatically.

Aura does not use iCloud or CloudKit for health information. Clear All Data removes local Aura records, documents, conversations, credentials, and preferences without altering Apple Health.

Aura is an organizational and educational wellness tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat medical conditions.
