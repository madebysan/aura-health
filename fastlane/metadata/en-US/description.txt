Aura keeps your vitals, lab results, medications, habits, conditions, and health notes together in one private iPhone dashboard.

READ YOUR TRENDS
Import selected Apple Health categories or add measurements manually. Review heart rate, HRV, sleep, steps, weight, blood pressure, oxygen saturation, temperature, and other records over time.

ORGANIZE LAB RESULTS
Track biomarkers across lab sessions, keep the original report in your local vault, and review educational context alongside each value. Every imported result remains editable before it is saved.

KEEP ROUTINES TOGETHER
Log habits, medications, supplements, conditions, and diet notes beside the measurements they may affect.

OPTIONAL AI CHAT
Choose Anthropic or OpenRouter and use your own API key. AI remains disabled until you review the provider disclosure and allow processing. Aura sends information only when you explicitly send a message or attachment.

LOCAL-FIRST PRIVACY
Aura has no account, advertising, analytics, or developer-owned health-data server. Your Aura database stays locally on your iPhone. You can create a complete backup or delete local data and credentials from Settings.

Aura is an organizational and educational wellness tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
