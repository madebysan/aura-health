# Aura Health Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 2, 2026

Aura Health is a local-first personal wellness application created by Santiago Alonso.

## Data stored on your device

Aura stores the health records, habits, medications, conditions, lab values, documents, preferences, and conversations you add in a local database on your iPhone. Aura does not operate an account system or developer-owned health-data server. Aura does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Aura reads only the Apple Health categories you choose to authorize. Apple Health permissions remain controlled by Apple in Settings and the Health app. Aura does not write data to Apple Health.

## Optional AI processing

AI features are optional and remain disabled until you select Anthropic or OpenRouter, supply your own API key, and explicitly allow processing by that provider.

When you send an AI message or attachment, Aura sends the content you selected and health records returned by its read tools for that request to the selected provider. Aura does not send health information automatically or in the background. Provider processing is governed by the provider's own terms and privacy policy:

- Anthropic: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy
- OpenRouter: https://openrouter.ai/privacy

API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain. Removing a provider key also revokes Aura's stored consent for that provider.

## Backups and deletion

Aura creates a backup only when you explicitly export one. The resulting file may contain sensitive health information and is handled by the destination you choose in the iOS share sheet.

Clear All Data removes Aura's local health records, documents, conversations, API keys, and preferences. It does not change or delete records stored in Apple Health.

## Medical disclaimer

Aura is an organizational and educational wellness tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or replace advice from a qualified healthcare professional.
