# Aura Health Support

Aura Health is an iPhone app for organizing personal vitals, lab results, medications, habits, conditions, and health notes.

## Before contacting support

- Confirm the iPhone is running a supported version of iOS.
- For Apple Health access, review Aura under **Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices**.
- For AI chat, confirm a provider, API key, model, and consent are configured under **Aura → Settings → AI**.
- Create a complete Aura backup before replacing or clearing local data.

Do not email medical records, API keys, or exported Aura backups.
